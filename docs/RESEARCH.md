# Market & procedure research — gap analysis

Web research (competitors, Garmin procedures, student and school needs, learning sites) compared with the app and docs/VISION.md.

## Summary

Gap audit of /home/user/ifr against docs/VISION.md, the code and four research reports. Status first: none of the VISION P0 files exist yet. src/ui/ has no profile.ts, today.ts, quickPlan.ts or result.ts, and src/train/ has no site.ts, mastery.ts, checkpoints.ts or nextUp.ts. The IFR Academy (academy.html → src/learn/main.ts) shows only 'IFR Academy — coming soon', and src/learn/topics is empty. The gaps below are additions to VISION or changes to it. They do not repeat its features: where one touches a VISION feature, it names that feature and says only what changes.

The owner's requests set the top priorities.
1. In-flight flight-plan changes are not realistic. FlightPlan.setHold() replaces the whole plan (flightplan.ts:112). A Direct-To to a fix outside the plan wipes the plan (flightplan.ts:131-145). The MFD FMS knob activates legs the moment it turns (main.ts:668). There is no insert, remove, OBS or pilot SUSP. The lesson engine programs the GPS for the student (circuits.ts:148/250, curriculum.ts:60/104, setup.ts:173).
2. The steam-cockpit GPS is a drawn 'GNS 430-like' display with no keys or knobs (steam.ts:438-463). D→ and PROC are G1000 bezel keys that open generic web modals.
3. The app does not work on iPad. The G1000 bezels are each sized from 50vw, there are no orientation layouts, maps zoom only with the mouse wheel, the touch stick is 44 px and springs back, there is no PWA, and VISION fixes the layout target at 1280×720.
4. The landing page (intro.ts) has one 'Open the Flight Center' button. VISION's P0 profile acceptance test says the first screen must have no 'Flight Center' button, so VISION needs amending.
5. The circuit builder's map only selects a leg (circuitBuilder.ts:1815). CircuitLeg has 4 step types. Failures trigger only by time or by leg number.
6. Flight schools have no assignment loop and no instructor station that works outside the student's browser.
7. The Academy is an empty shell.

The research says that no competitor grades how the pilot manages the box under ATC amendments, that Garmin's own trainers have no flight model, and that browser and mobile competitors are 2D or desktop-only. That makes items 1-3 the main differentiators.

## Gaps

### P0

**Garmin-faithful flight-plan core: insert/remove, Direct-To that keeps the plan, holds inside the plan, OBS/SUSP, Activate Leg with confirmation** (Nav core (src/nav/flightplan.ts, src/sim/sim.ts, tests/flightplan.test.ts). This is a new package that VISION lacks; Training and Cockpit systems depend on it.)  
Evidence: Code: setHold() sets this.wpts=[{...fix, hold}] and throws away the route and approach (flightplan.ts:112-121). directTo() to a fix outside the plan sets wpts=[target] and clears approachId (l.131-145), but a real G1000 or GNS keeps the stored plan. activateLeg(i) takes effect at once with no confirmation (l.233). There is no insertBefore/remove/removeApproach. The PFD's first softkey is blank, so there is no OBS (pfd.ts:16 PFD_SOFTKEYS[0]==='') and the pilot cannot clear SUSP (out.suspended is set internally, l.~285-305). vectorsToFinal() keeps every pre-FAF fix (l.208), which is neither GNS nor GTN behaviour. Sources: G1000 NXi Pilot's Guide 190-02177-00 §5 (Direct-To, Activate Leg, insert before cursor, CLR 'Remove XXXXX?', three hold kinds, OBS/SUSP, PROC Activate Missed); Garmin AXIS hold-in-flight-plan webhelp (SUSP at the hold fix until Exit Hold, hold timer starts inbound); GNS 430W guide p.32 and PilotWorkshops VTF tip (GNS deletes pre-FAF fixes, GTN keeps on-course ones); the owner's request for real Direct-To, activate leg, load/activate/VTF and holds.  
Design: Extend FlightPlan. All new members are optional so the other packages still compile.
(1) insertBefore(i, wpt), remove(i), removeApproach(), replaceApproach(id, trans).
(2) activateLeg(i) becomes requestActivateLeg(i) → pending:{kind:'actLeg', i}, confirm() and cancel(). Shell and UI drive the confirmation prompt.
(3) directTo(target, from, {course?, vnvAlt?}). If target is outside the plan, keep wpts in a stored 'resume' state and set offRoute={target}. cancelDirect() resumes the plan along the closest leg.
(4) The hold becomes a waypoint property: hold:{inbound, turn, legMin|legNm, efc?, kind:'published'|'user', exitRequested}. Add holdAt(i, spec) and holdPresentPosition(spec), which places the fix 0.3 NM ahead of the aircraft. Turn setHold() into a thin wrapper that calls holdAt on an inserted waypoint, keeping the old signature for callers. Add exitHold() (SUSP), which flies to the fix on the inbound course and then sequences to the next waypoint.
(5) OBS: obsMode and obsCourse give TO/FROM steering through the active waypoint with no sequencing; syncObs() sets the course to the current bearing.
(6) unsuspend(): at the MAP it calls activateMissed(); in a hold it calls exitHold(). A HILPT suspends for one turn only.
(7) Box profile flags {vtfDropsPreFaf:boolean} (GNS and older G1000 true, GTN false), used by vectorsToFinal().
(8) Every edit records an event {t, op, args} in sim.recorder for grading and debrief.  
Acceptance: Vitest:
(a) A plan A-B-C plus the KPAO ILS. directTo(off-route X) keeps wpts.length unchanged, and cancelDirect() makes the leg into B or C active, whichever is closest.
(b) insertBefore(2, Y) then remove(2) restores the original idents, and active stays on the same ident.
(c) holdAt(idx of B, {inbound:270, turn:'R', legMin:1}) keeps C and the approach after B. After 2 circuits, exitHold() sequences to C.
(d) requestActivateLeg(3) leaves active unchanged until confirm().
(e) With obsMode on and course 090, crossing the waypoint does not sequence, and toFrom flips to FROM.
(f) At the MAP, unsuspend() makes missedActive true.
(g) With vtfDropsPreFaf true, vectorsToFinal() leaves no appr:'trans' waypoints; with it false, they stay.
(h) Every existing flightplan, holding, circuits and curriculum test still passes.

**Make the student program the box: no auto-programming in Solo/Checkride, plus graded 'box tasks' after each ATC clearance** (Training (src/train/engine.ts, circuits.ts, curriculum.ts, setup.ts) and a new src/train/boxTask.ts. Amends VISION P0 'One help switch' and the P1 'Next up' strip.)  
Evidence: Code: the circuit approach step calls sim.loadApproach(..., 'activate'|'vtf') itself (circuits.ts:148). Holds call fp.setHold automatically (circuits.ts:250, curriculum.ts:60, 503). Planner flights preload the approach (setup.ts:173), and sim.loadApproach also tunes NAV1, sets the OBS and sets the minimums (sim.ts:238-253). The GPS work under ATC, which is the main workload on a checkride, is therefore never practised. Sources: AOPA 2022 sim tips ('most simulated GPS knobology is poor'); AOPA Glass Class (finger placement is the biggest glass hurdle); Rotate Pilot checkride guide (GPS not set up for the miss is a common bust); PilotWorkshops 'Activate Approach' tip (Activate does not arm APR); research finding that no product grades programming the box in response to an ATC amendment.  
Design: New boxTask.ts: expect(sim, spec):BoxCheck. spec is {approachId?, transition?|'vtf', activeTo?, holdAt?{ident, inbound, turn}, cdi?, apr?, navFreq?, minimums?, withinSec}. It returns {met:boolean, late:boolean, wrong:string[]}, using FlightPlan state and recorder edit events.
Each clearance step in circuits.ts and curriculum.ts gets box?:BoxSpec. In Coached mode the engine still auto-programs, but only after a 20 s grace period. Until then it shows the exact keystroke chain as a coach chip, chosen by cockpit profile: G1000 'PROC › Activate Vector-to-Final › ENT'; GNS 430W 'PROC › Select Approach › VECTORS › Activate'.
In Solo and Checkride nothing is programmed for the student. sim.loadApproach(...) splits into fp-only and avionicsSetup(), and avionicsSetup runs only in Coached.
The result screen (VISION ResultModal) gets a line such as 'Box: approach loaded 40 s after clearance · wrong transition (VTF instead of TOBEY)' with code IR.III.A.
nextUp.ts (VISION P1) reads open BoxChecks first.  
Acceptance: Vitest:
(a) A circuit approach leg in mode 'solo': after the clearance event, sim.fp.approachId stays null until the test calls the PROC API. BoxCheck reports late=true once withinSec is exceeded.
(b) The same leg in 'training' (Coached) loads the approach after 20 s, and coachChip contains 'Activate Vector-to-Final'.
(c) The student loads transition 'ARD' where the spec says 'vtf': wrong contains 'transition'.
Headless: in a Solo ILS lesson, flying to the FAF without loading the approach gives a result Details row 'Box' marked not met. grep shows no unconditional sim.loadApproach( call in circuits.ts outside the coached branch.

**ATC amendment step types generated from JO 7110.65 rules (direct-to IF/IAF, vectors to final, change approach, hold as published / present position / EFC, cross-at, VTF trap)** (Training and Planner (src/train/circuits.ts CircuitLeg, src/ui/circuitBuilder.ts, src/ui/atcRadio.ts). Builds on VISION P1 'Read-back' (readback.ts) and P2 'Instructor station presets'.)  
Evidence: Code: CircuitLeg is only fix | alt | hold | approach (circuits.ts:16-20). ATC only transmits (atcRadio.ts say(), l.201). There is no step that amends a clearance mid-flight. Sources: JO 7110.65 4-8 (direct to an IAF/IF at up to 90° intercept, or a fix between IF and FAF at up to 30°, with 'expect direct' at least 5 NM before); JO 7110.65 5-9 (vectors join at least 2 NM outside the approach gate, 20° intercept within 2 NM of the gate and 30° beyond, with canonical phraseology); AIM 5-4-7/5-4-9 (maintain the last assigned altitude until established; cleared to a holding fix and then the approach means via the hold fix); AOPA 'Where's that fix?' (cleared 'cross JANUD at or above…' after VTF removed JANUD); MSFS forums (sim ATC gives no vectors and abandons you after a missed); the owner's request for ATC amendments.  
Design: Add CircuitLeg types:
{type:'amendDirect', ident, crossAlt?}
{type:'vectors', approachId, joinNm:number /* ≥2 outside gate */, maintain:number}
{type:'changeApproach', approachId, transition|null, reason:'runway change'|'wind'}
{type:'holdPublished', ident, efcMin}
{type:'holdPpos', inbound, turn, legMin, efcMin}
{type:'crossAt', ident, alt, constraint:'at'|'atOrAbove'}
{type:'expectThen', text, thenLeg}
An amendment generator, amendments.ts in Training, checks legality before issuing. amendDirect is issued only when the intercept angle is ≤90° (IAF/IF) or ≤30° (IF-FAF), and an 'expect direct X' call is sent at ≥5 NM. Vectors are flown as a scripted ATC heading series that ends with '<n> miles from <FAF>, turn heading X, maintain Y until established, cleared ILS RWY Z approach', using an intercept of 20° or 30° depending on distance to the gate.
Each amendment carries a BoxSpec (previous gap) and needsReadback (VISION readback.ts). The 'VTF trap' preset combines a changeApproach to VTF with crossAt on a pre-FAF fix and grades whether the student reloaded via the transition.
ATC always gives the published missed instructions after a missed approach; list this as a selling point.
The circuit builder gets an 'ATC events' lane in which these steps are placed at a fix, at a leg, or after N minutes.  
Acceptance: Vitest:
(a) amendDirect to the IF with the aircraft 50° off the final course is issued. At a 45° offset to a fix between the IF and FAF, the generator refuses and returns reason /30°/.
(b) A vectors leg produces a final heading whose intercept with the final course is ≤30°, with join distance ≥2 NM outside the gate. The transmitted text matches /miles from .* maintain \d+ until established, cleared/.
(c) holdPpos yields a FlightPlan hold at present position with EFC set, and a radio event of kind 'efc' fires at efcMin.
Headless: the 'VTF trap' preset says 'cross JANUD at or above 3000'. If the student keeps the VTF plan, the result shows 'Box: JANUD not in flight plan'.

Ground-training software is the recommended fix.

- [Flight Safety Foundation: Use it or lose it (Casner et al. 2014 on skill retention)](https://flightsafety.org/asw-article/use-it-or-lose-it/): Scanning and manual control resist decay even after months off. Cognitive and procedural skills decay faster: recalling procedure steps, tracking position, automation-dependent knowledge. Pilots overrate how much skill they have kept.
- [Cepeda, Pashler et al. 2006, Distributed practice meta-analysis](https://www.evullab.org/pdf/CepedaPashlerVulWixtedRohrer-PB-2006.pdf): Spaced practice beats massed practice for retention, and the best gap grows with how long the skill must be kept. Supports short, spaced sessions over a big session the night before the lesson.
- [Lazyweb: How Duolingo used streak goals to improve retention](https://experiments.lazyweb.com/research/duolingo-streak-goals-retention): Anecdotal product write-up: users who actively chose a streak goal kept going longer, even though the app never used the number they picked, so making the commitment did the work. Critics say streaks reward showing up more than learning.

- [Flying: Approach briefings](https://www.flyingmag.com/?p=43836): Two phases. 'Build it': set avionics, frequencies, courses and minimums early. 'Brief it': say aloud the plate, decision points and threats. Always plan for the miss.
