# Product vision — the easiest way for instrument students to practise IFR

Synthesised from three independent reviews of the app: a new instrument student, a CFII, and a learning-science / game-UX designer.

## Vision

Our app is the most intuitive way to practise IFR because a student can go from "I have 30 minutes tonight" to a coached, graded attempt at the hold, ILS or partial-panel task they will fly with their CFI on Thursday, at their own airport on the real FAA procedure, in under a minute and with one click. Nothing else on the market gets them there: MSFS and X-Plane need 20 minutes of setup and never grade the hold entry, and ground-school apps never let you fly it. The flying is already pro-grade: G1000, steam and airliner panels, CIFP approaches and holds for about 2,000 US airports, the AIM hold-entry evaluator, ACS tolerances, ATIS, replay and failures. What stands in the way is the layer around the flying. Today there are five front doors, an 8-step wizard, four mode switches, two result screens with two scores, and a syllabus that lives only in the fictional Ardent Islands. So the plan starts with subtraction. The student sets a home airport once. One "Today" screen shows one button, Fly next lesson, and every lesson runs at that home airport. A 30-second briefing leads to a quiet cockpit with five controls and one help switch (Coached, Solo, Checkride) that fades as the student improves. Then we add what a good CFI does and desktop sims don't: say what is coming next, show how much tolerance is left and how to correct, make the student read back clearances, cover the instruments, and let them rewind 30 seconds. Every flight ends on one result screen that names the one thing to fix and offers a one-click rep to practise it, so each mistake leads straight to the next rep.

## Design principles (every screen)

- One primary action per screen. Everything else sits one level deeper, behind 'Browse all flights', 'Customize…', 'Details' or 'More', and the same thing never appears twice under two names.
- Real world first. The home airport and its published CIFP procedures are the default everywhere. The fictional Ardent Islands appear only as a clearly labelled fallback ('Training region': offline, outside the US, or when the procedure isn't published near home).
- Intent to airborne in 3 clicks or fewer and under 60 seconds. Any retry takes at most 1 click: no re-briefing, no re-answering a quiz, no re-flying the transit legs.
- Feedback comes while the student is still flying, and it is corrective ('Descend 120 ft', '9 s of tolerance left'), not descriptive. Help fades with competence: Coached → Solo → Checkride.
- Every flight ends with one result screen, one score and one named fix, plus a one-click way to practise exactly that ('Watch it' / 'Practise it').
- Quiet, pro-grade cockpit. The instruments dominate. The top bar has at most 5 labelled controls, every action has a keyboard shortcut, layouts work at 1280×720 and on a projector, and the UI uses the app's own modals, never native confirm().
- Honest and standards-based. The app uses real phraseology and ACS tolerances and language, shows no score or letter grade for ungraded flights, never implies browser time is loggable, and Checkride mode really has no help.
- Cross-package APIs are additive. New hooks and fields are optional, so each package compiles on its own. Only the owning package deletes an old export, and it does so in the same integration step where the Shell package stops importing it.

## Features

### P0

**Student profile with home airport (picked once, used everywhere)**

It is stored in localStorage 'ifr.profile.v1' (one active profile in P0, wrapped in try/catch). First run (no profile) replaces the intro's 3 CTAs with one card, 'Where do you train?'. The card has an airport search using searchAirports() from src/nav/world.ts, a 'Use my location' button (navigator.geolocation → nearbyAirports(lat,lon,5), result list), three panel chips (G1000 / Six-pack / Airliner, default G1000), an optional 'Your name', and a ghost link 'Skip, use the training region' (homeIcao=null). Enter confirms. Effects: (1) the intro is never shown again once a profile exists, and the app opens on Today. (2) A brand pill 'KPAO ▾' in the Flight Center header and the Shell top bar opens the same picker to change it. (3) The Shell drops launcher.assess.student and uses profile.name for sim.assess.student. (4) Brief&Debrief keys exercise progress as 'ifr.progress.v1:<profile.id>' and copies the legacy 'ifr.progress.v1' into the first profile once. (5) The Planner pre-fills dep/arr, cockpit and equipment from the profile. (6) After the picker the Shell calls loadRegion([homeIcao]) in the background (the same call the wizard uses) so the first lesson starts without waiting. Remove the 'Student name' input from the flightcenter.ts detail pane and the Flight Center cockpit picker; the panel lives in the profile and Settings.  
Acceptance: Headless (fresh localStorage): the first screen contains exactly one input with placeholder 'Search airport or city', a 'Use my location' button and a 'Skip' link, and no 'Flight Center' or 'Start a flight' buttons. Type 'KPAO', pick the first row, press Enter: localStorage 'ifr.profile.v1' parses to homeIcao==='KPAO', and the next screen is the Today home showing a 'KPAO' pill. **Syllabus lessons at your real home airport**  
Why: All 44 graded exercises are hard-wired to fictional fixes (F('PARD'), navaid('ARD'), runway('PARD','27')), and flying from the Flight Center calls leaveRealWorld(). That contradicts the owner's real-world-first rule and stops practice from transferring to the student's next real lesson.  
Design: Training creates src/train/site.ts: export interface Site {kind:'real'|'training'; icao:string; runway:Runway; vor?:Navaid; ndb?:Navaid; holds:PublishedHold[]; approaches:Record<'ils'|'loc'|'rnav'|'lnavvnav'|'vor'|'vordme'|'ndb'|'bc'|'lda'|'circle', Approach|undefined>}. resolveSite(icao|null, onProgress?) runs loadRegion([icao]) (src/nav/world.ts) and then indexes APPROACHES/NAVAIDS/FIXES. If the network fails, the airport is outside the CIFP set or icao is null, it returns the training site built from TRAINING_REGION. Also: exerciseAt(id, site): Exercise & {where:string}, and publishedHolds(site) (moved here from flightsetup.ts; the Planner then imports it and deletes its copy). Refactor curriculum.ts so the module-level constants (ARD, MSV, PA, r27, OPEN) become fields of a site argument, and the factories holdExercise/approachExercise/interceptExercise/basics take (site) => Exercise. EXERCISES stays exported as the training-site build so existing tests and ids keep working. Resolution rules: holds use a published hold (missed-approach hold or HILPT) or, if there is none, a hold at the nearest VOR on the radial toward the airport. For hold-direct/teardrop/parallel the start course is chosen so that holdEntry(course, inbound, turn) equals the lesson's entry (sample courses in 10° steps). Approaches pick the airport's procedure of the required type. If it is missing, use the nearest airport within 60 NM with that type (nearbyAirports + FLAG_PROC). Otherwise fall back to the training site, with where = 'Training region · NDB approach not published near KPAO'. Intercepts use the home ILS/LOC final course. Basic attitude and partial-panel exercises use startAt 12 NM from home at the nearest 500 ft above 3000 AGL. Progress stays keyed by exercise id wherever it was flown. Brief&Debrief shows the loadRegion progress line in the briefing while the site resolves. The Shell calls exerciseAt instead of exercise(id) for Today/catalog launches, and calls backToTraining() only when site.kind==='training'.  
Acceptance: Vitest using public/navdata/proc/KPAO.json (and KAUS.json): resolveSite('KPAO') returns kind 'real' with approaches.ils or approaches.rnav defined. exerciseAt('hold-teardrop', site).runtime hold fix is a real KPAO-region fix, and holdEntry(startCourse, inbound, turn)==='teardrop' (the same holds for 'hold-direct' and 'hold-parallel'). exerciseAt('app-ndb-pt', site).where matches /not published|Training region/ or names an airport ≤60 NM away. resolveSite(null).kind==='training' and EXERCISES ids are unchanged. Screenshot: the briefing for 'app-ils-hand' with a KPAO profile shows an 'At KPAO' chip and the real KPAO plate, and the outside view shows the real runway at start.

**One help switch: Coached · Solo · Checkride, fading with success**  
Why: Four overlapping switches (Pro mode, Graded/Free, Training/Checkride, Hand-flown/Random failure) confuse students. All-or-nothing help builds dependence before the checkride.  
Design: Training creates src/train/mastery.ts with export type HelpLevel='coached'|'solo'|'checkride' and suggestHelp(p:Progress): HelpLevel. Two passes at Coached suggest Solo, two passes at Solo suggest Checkride, and any fail suggests one level down. Systems extends AssessSettings.mode in sim.ts to 'training'|'solo'|'checkride' ('training' = Coached, kept for compatibility) and derives handFlown from checkride + !ex.allowAp. Meaning: Coached = coach panel, hint, Next-up strip, corrective cues, rewind. Solo = step title + tolerance budget only, no hints/toasts, rewind allowed, graded silently. Checkride = no coach, no rewind, ACS pass/fail, AP inhibited unless the task allows it, and an optional 'Surprise failure' checkbox (the old randomFailure). The same 3-segment control, labelled 'Help', appears in the briefing (Brief&Debrief), Quick Plan/Customize (Planner) and Settings as the default for free flights (Shell; it replaces 'Pro mode'). The Planner's 'Graded vs Free practice' becomes a flight type ('Free flight, no grading'), not a mode. Brief&Debrief stores per exercise in Progress {passesAt:{coached,solo,checkride}, lastHelp}, and the result shows 'Passed in Coached, next time: Solo'. The Shell's updateCoach reads sim.assess.mode instead of proMode, and the 'ifr.pro' key migrates to profile.help='solo'.  
Acceptance: `grep -rn "proMode\|'ifr.pro'" src` returns nothing after integration. Vitest: suggestHelp({passesAt:{coached:2,solo:0,checkride:0}, lastFail:false})==='solo', and after a fail at solo it returns 'coached'. Headless: start any lesson with mode 'solo'. The coach DOM shows the step title and a budget bar but no element .coach-hint, and no info toasts are produced. With mode 'checkride' #coach is hidden and pressing Backspace (rewind) shows 'Rewind is off in Checkride'. Screenshot: the briefing, Quick Plan and Settings all show the same 'Help: Coached | Solo | Checkride' control, and the Settings screen has no 'Pro mode' row.

**30-second briefing: goal, key numbers, plate, Fly**

**One result screen: one score, 'Fix next', Watch it, Practise it**  
Why: Lessons end on LessonResultModal and then DebriefModal, with two different scores (engine 62 vs computeDebrief 'B 85'). Neither names the single fix, jumps to the moment it went wrong, or offers the drill that trains it.  
Design: Brief&Debrief creates src/ui/result.ts (ResultModal) and retires LessonResultModal, plus DebriefModal's standalone use for lessons. Tabs: Summary | Details | Export. Summary has: (1) a large 'Satisfactory' / 'Not yet' result. It comes from LessonResult.passed for lessons, from the ACS for checkride, and is omitted for ungraded free flights. Below it, ONE 0-100 score: the engine LessonResult score for any graded flight (lessons and planner flights via circuitToExercise). computeDebrief's score and A-F letter are no longer displayed, and ungraded flights show no score at all. Then the help level and 'rewound 2×'. (2) 'Went well': up to 2 steps with 0 s out of tolerance. (3) 'Fix next': the worst item (the step with most violation seconds, a failed ACS item, or the top recorder tip), with its worst deviation and the hint text. It has a [Watch it] button (onWatch(t−3 s): Shell calls startReplay and seeks) and a [Practise it · 6 min] button (onPractise). A FIX_MAP in result.ts maps tip/step ids to an exercise or drill: lateral → int-loc30 / nav-vor-in, vertical → app-ils-hand, segment floor → app-vor-arc, wrong hold entry → hold drill, not stabilized → ma-da. Per failed step it also offers 'Retry from step N'. Primary button: 'Next lesson' after a pass, 'Retry' (no briefing) after a fail. 'Keep flying' and 'New flight' sit in a secondary menu. Details holds today's ACS table, step table, ground track + vertical profile and timeline. Export holds today's CSV/PDF/webhook buttons. API: ResultModal.show({sim, run|null, path, onNext, onRetry, onRetryStep(i), onWatch(t), onPractise(exId|'drill:hold')}).  
Acceptance: Headless: finish a lesson with a forced failure (window.__ifr hooks). Exactly one modal is open, its Summary contains 'Not yet', one numeric score and a 'Fix next' block with 'Watch it' and 'Practise it' buttons, and no element shows a letter grade /\b[A-F]\b · \d+/. 'Watch it' sets sim.replaying=true with replay time within 3 s before the first violation timestamp. Free ungraded flight: the Summary has no score element. Vitest: fixNext() over a synthetic LessonResult picks the step with the largest violationSec and maps a 'lateral' tip to 'int-loc30'. `grep -rn LessonResultModal src` returns nothing.

**Rewind 30 s and 'Retry this step' checkpoints**  
Why: After blowing a teardrop entry 12 minutes into a lesson, the only options are a full restart from the briefing or 'Retry this flight' on the crash veil. That kills deliberate practice.  
Design: Training creates src/train/checkpoints.ts: class Checkpoints {constructor(sim, getRun); tick(dt); rewind(sec=30):boolean; retryStep(i):boolean; rewinds:number}. Every 5 s, and at every lesson step start, it snapshots a deep copy of sim.ac.s, fp (active leg index, approach/missed flags, hold state), av radios/OBS/CDI source/bearing pointers, ap modes and bugs, sim.time, the env wind seed, and the LessonRun's idx/stepT/mem/holdEval/violation accumulators (LessonRun exposes snapshot()/restore() in engine.ts). The ring buffer holds 3 min and step checkpoints are kept for the run. Restoring truncates recorder samples/events after the snapshot time, so the debrief track stays continuous, and resumes paused for a 3-2-1 countdown. The violation seconds of the rewound span are removed, but LessonResult records rewinds and retriedSteps, which are shown on the result. The Shell wires Backspace = Rewind 30 s and R = Retry step, adds 'Rewind 30 s' to the coach panel, the crash veil (next to 'Retry flight') and the flight menu, and connects the result's 'Retry from step N'. It is disabled in Checkride.  
Acceptance: Vitest: build a LessonRun on 'hold-teardrop', advance the sim 120 s, call rewind(30), and get sim.time≈90±5, run.idx equal to the value at t=90, and the recorder's last sample t≤90. retryStep(1) restores the state captured at step 1 start, and run.result().rewinds===1. Headless: during a lesson press Backspace. Aircraft position changes back toward the earlier track, a '3' countdown overlay appears, and the coach shows 'Rewound 30 s'. In checkride mode Backspace has no effect and shows a toast. The crash veil screenshot shows 'Rewind 30 s' as the primary button.

### P1

**'Next up' strip: stay ahead of the airplane**  
Why: The coach only warns once something is already wrong ('Switch the CDI to LOC1 before the FAF' at dFaf<12). Falling behind the airplane is the most common approach failure for instrument students.  
Design: Training creates src/train/nextUp.ts: nextUp(sim, run|null): NextItem[] (≤3) with {at:string /* '2.1 NM' | '0:45' */, fix?:string, action:string, done:boolean, dueSec:number}. It is built from fp.wpts and the active approach: altitude constraints at upcoming fixes ('KEDDY: descend 2400'), CDI source/APR arm before the FAF ('CDI → LOC1, arm APR'), the FAF action ('GS intercept, start timer, flaps 10'), the DA/MDA decision ('at DA 270: runway or go-around (G)'), the missed-approach first action, and the hold entry ('Hold entry decision', see the hold-entry feature). For lessons, the next 2 steps' titles are used when no flight-plan items apply.
