## 2. Work packages (owned files are disjoint)

Rules: no two packages own the same file. Cross-package APIs are additive and optional, so each package compiles on its own. **PKG-4 Shell is the only integration point and the sole owner of `src/main.ts`.** Every package's first PR contains only its P0 fixes, so the stop-ship bugs merge on day 1.

### PKG-1 Lesson engine truth & corrective coach (Training)

**Goal:** Every graded step checks what it claims to check, and the coach says how to correct, not just what is wrong.

**Owns:** `src/train/engine.ts`, `src/train/setupAssist.ts`, `src/train/holdEval.ts`, `src/train/diagram.ts`, `src/train/nextUp.ts` (new), `src/train/checkpoints.ts` (new), `tests/engine.test.ts` (new), `tests/checkpoints.test.ts` (new), `tests/nextUp.test.ts` (new), `tests/setup-assist.test.ts`, `tests/diagram.test.ts`

**Fixes:**

- **W2-10 (P0).** In `LessonRun.update`, after `onStart()` either rebuild `ctx` or return before `done()`. `done()` also needs `stepT > 1 s`.
- **W2-18 (P0).** Add `LessonRun.awayFromFix()`, which fires when the distance to the hold fix grows for more than 20 s before the fix crossing. `setupAssist` gets the item "AP NAV coupled to a source that leads to the fix", so "All set" no longer shows while the coupling is wrong. holdEval reports "fix not reached: flew away from ARD on CRS 270".
- **W2-12.** `devCheck` returns `Dev[]` ordered by excess over tolerance, each `{param, err, text, cue}` (for example 'Descend 360 ft' or 'Turn right to 090 (176° off)'). Also add `budget()`, `current()` and the 'Press ALT or VS' cue when the AP is in PIT mode.
- **W2-19.** Freeze the planned entry within 3 NM, or once the hold is sequenced, taking it from `fp.out.holdEntry`. Recompute only beyond 5 NM.
- **W2-04 (engine part).** `LessonResult.graded:false` plus a `reason` when 0 steps were completed, less than 60 s was flown, or the FAF was not crossed. Add `stepPassed` per row.

## 3. Top 5 differentiators to build (and who carries them)

1. **"Your lesson, at your airport, on Thursday's procedure."** The graded syllabus relocates to the student's real home airport (CIFP holds and approaches, about 2,000 US airports), with the training region as a labelled fallback. No browser or iPad competitor has it. Navigraph Academy and INTERCEPTOR are MSFS-only; GIFT needs Redbird hardware. **Carrier: PKG-2** (site.ts, profile, Today), helped by PKG-1 (engine runs on site data).
2. **A 20-minute currency or approach rep, airborne in 3 clicks.** Start at the IAF or on vectors at the home airport, with GPS sequencing that is right after any reposition, live METAR, and a 'Currency set' template (hold plus 2–3 approach types). **Carrier: PKG-6** (start options, template), helped by PKG-5 (sequencing, live weather).
3. **A CFI's feedback loop.** In flight the coach gives corrective cues with a tolerance budget ('Descend 120 ft · 4 s left'). Every flight ends on one result screen with one named fix, plus 'Watch it' and 'Practise just this', and rewind 30 s. **Carrier: PKG-3** (result), with PKG-1 (cues, budget, worst deviation, checkpoints) and PKG-4 (render).
4. **A checkride mode an examiner would recognise.** It gives no help, flies no configuration for the pilot, and produces an ACS PASS/FAIL report over the whole flight (hold, en-route, missed, MDA +100/−0), with a one-click mock checkride at the home airport. **Carrier: PKG-3** (ACS), with PKG-4 (no-help wiring), PKG-2 (category, `ex.mode`) and PKG-6 (t-acs).
5. **The school assignment loop with no backend.** The CFI builds or picks a lesson, assigns it to a named student, and sends a link or QR. The student opens a briefing card, not an editor. The result lands in the roster and dashboard, and records open the debrief. **Carrier: PKG-6** (school.ts, #circuit briefing, presets and packs), with PKG-3 (history payload and import).

Next in line for Wave 3: voice readback (CRAFT), graded box work under ATC amendments, and a hosted pairing relay plus cloud sync for schools.

## 4. Honest verdict per persona

**Student (Lea).** She is airborne in 30 s and 4 clicks, and the briefing, the hold-entry diagram and the drill are genuinely good teaching material. Her first real minute in the cockpit goes to a Flight Center tour while the graded clock runs. The coach tells her what is wrong, not what to do, and shows one deviation when she has two. On the hold the coach shows a green 'All set' for 8 minutes while the autopilot carries her 10 NM away from the fix. Two result screens then give her two verdicts and no reason. Today this is a well-built simulator with a grader that is sometimes wrong and a coach that does not yet teach. It is not safe to hand to a student unsupervised until PKG-1, PKG-3 and PKG-4 land. After them it becomes the coached practice tool VISION describes.

**CFI school (Marc).** The flight builder is the best authoring surface in this market: route strings with inline errors, a vertical profile with terrain, failures pinned to legs, QR sharing without a server, and an excellent phone companion. One saved real-world flight blacks out the app on every reload, which is a stop-ship for a school running 5 shared PCs. The Vectors chip breaks valid lessons, SID and STAR picks go 200 NM into the Pacific, and nothing warns him about a route below terrain. There is no school preset, roster, assignment state or record that leaves the browser. Today it is a great tool for a ground lesson with the phone remote, but it cannot be deployed as the school's homework system. PKG-2 (the P0), PKG-6 and the PKG-3 history work make it one.

**Currency pilot (Sofia).** The realism she cares about is there: real CIFP data, a Garmin-faithful Direct-To and hold window, the CDI auto-switching to LOC, GFC700 captures and an amber MINIMUMS call. She cannot cash it in, though. It takes 11 clicks and 2 minutes to reach practice, there is no home airport, and the default local ILS is a 1-hour flight. 'Airborne' spawns her 20 NM away and abeam the FAF. A reposition to the IF sends the autopilot back toward a fix 25 NM behind, and an aborted 14 s flight is graded 70/100. For a 20-minute weekly rep it currently costs more setup than MSFS. PKG-6 start options, PKG-5 sequencing and PKG-2 home airport turn her into the easiest user to win and keep.

**Checkride applicant (Tom).** The ACS table has the right shape, and a real MDA bust was correctly failed. Almost everything else gives him false confidence, which is the worst failure for a checkride-prep tool. The only 'checkride' flight is coached. The unusual-attitude lesson skips the nose-low spiral and scores it 100/100. A FAIL sits under a big blue 'B 80'. The airspeed standard is his own median speed, TOGA sets power and flaps for him, the report grades only the final segment, a LOC approach is drawn with a glidepath, and there is no circling. Until PKG-1 and PKG-3 ship and PKG-4 removes the help, the app should not use the word 'checkride' in its marketing.

**Market / differentiation.** The core asset is real and unmatched at $0 in a browser. It has the real FAA CIFP at about 2,067 airports, FAA-style plates with own-ship, QR instructor pairing with no install, ForeFlight-compatible exports, and the fastest intent-to-practice in the field (3 clicks, 14 s). The product layer hides that asset. The only thing that teaches, the 44-lesson syllabus, runs at a fictional airport. Weather is synthetic. Progress and records stay in one browser, pairing depends on a public broker with no failure state, and the Academy tile opens a blank page. The 'homework sim for schools' position (free for students at their own airport, a paid seat for assignments and a CFI dashboard) is credible, but only after the syllabus runs at the home airport (PKG-2) and the assignment loop exists (PKG-6). Wave 2 should spend nothing on pricing pages until the P0s are gone.
